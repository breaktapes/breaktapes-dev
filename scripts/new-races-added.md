## ⚠️ Data artifacts worth a follow-up cleanup
1. **3 "DO-NOT-USE (see notes)" entries** — competitor.com internal placeholders that leaked in: `IRONMAN Kalmar-Sweden DO-NOT-USE`, `IRONMAN 70.3 Mallorca DO-NOT-USE`, `IRONMAN 70.3 Vichy DO-NOT-USE` (all 2023). Should be deleted.
2. **Accent/hyphen spelling splits** — same race under two names across years (competitor.com's own inconsistency): Jonkoping/Jönköping, Porec/Poreč, Sao Paulo/São Paulo, Pays d'Aix/Pays D'Aix, Mont Tremblant/Mont-Tremblant, Punta del Este/Punta Del Este, Turkey/Türkiye, Subic Bay/Subic Bay Philippines, Oceanside/Oceanside California. Canonicalization didn't merge accents/hyphens.
3. **"Triathlon" suffix (2002-2013)** and **"Bike-Run" (aquabike)** form separate groups from the base race — historically accurate but splits older years from the modern name.
